University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples — Steinway & Sons Model B
Recordings by the University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios, Lawrence Fritts.
https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MISpiano.html
https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html

The source's permission statement, checked 2026-10-26:
"may be downloaded and used for any projects, without restrictions."

These MP3 derivatives remove leading silence, retain up to twelve seconds of
decay and add an ending fade. Three original dynamic layers (pp, mf, ff), sampled
at minor-third intervals from C1 through C8, plus B0, preserve the recorded dynamics.
Other pitches use the nearest recorded key. Sources and derivative SHA256 hashes
are recorded in sources.json. Rebuild with scripts/prepare-piano-samples.py.

This is an independently recorded sample library, not an official Steinway product.
